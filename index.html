Everything you need to know about Emsculpt NEO in Milwaukee

While CoolSculpting only reduces fat through freezing, Emsculpt NEO does both - it builds muscle AND burns fat simultaneously in a single 30-minute treatment. CoolSculpting can't build muscle, and you can't treat muscle-building areas like arms or calves with it. Emsculpt NEO uses radiofrequency heating and electromagnetic energy to achieve results that CoolSculpting alone cannot provide. If you want a more toned, sculpted appearance - not just fat reduction - Emsculpt NEO is the superior choice.

Emsculpt NEO pricing varies based on the number of treatment areas and your individual goals. Most clients achieve optimal results with a series of 4 treatments per area. During your free consultation at our Bay View location, we'll create a customized treatment plan and provide transparent pricing. We serve patients from Milwaukee, Wauwatosa, Brookfield, and surrounding areas. Call (414) 295-6045 for your personalized quote.

The recommended treatment protocol is 4 sessions per area, scheduled 5-10 days apart. Each session takes just 30 minutes. Clinical studies show an average of 30% fat reduction and 25% muscle increase after the full series. Some clients choose additional treatments for enhanced results or maintenance sessions every few months.

No downtime whatsoever! Emsculpt NEO is completely non-invasive. You can return to work, exercise, and all normal activities immediately after your 30-minute session. Some clients experience mild muscle soreness similar to an intense workout, but this typically resolves within 24-48 hours. It's the perfect lunchtime treatment for busy Milwaukee professionals.

We can treat multiple areas including abdomen, buttocks, arms, hips, inner thighs, and flanks (love handles). Our practice has all three applicator sizes - large, small, and edge - allowing us to precisely target any treatment area. During your consultation, we'll assess which areas will benefit most from treatment based on your body sculpting goals.

Emsculpt NEO works for a wide range of body types. Ideal candidates have a BMI of 35 or below and want to build muscle while reducing fat in specific areas. It's perfect for individuals who are close to their goal weight but have stubborn areas that won't respond to diet and exercise. The treatment is not recommended for pregnant women or individuals with metal implants in the treatment area. Schedule a consultation to determine if you're a candidate.
