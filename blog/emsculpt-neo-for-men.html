Emsculpt NEO for Men:
Does It Work Differently?

By Bay View Chiropractic  ·  5 min read

← Back to Blog

Most Emsculpt NEO marketing features women. That's a business decision, not a medical one — the treatment works on male physiology exactly the same way it works on female physiology. At Bay View Chiropractic, men account for a meaningful share of our patients, and many of them came in skeptical that this was even an option for them.

If you've been curious but assumed the treatment wasn't really aimed at you, here's a straightforward breakdown of what to expect.

What Men Typically Come In For

Abdominal definition is the most common goal, by a significant margin. Men who are already training — who have real muscle underneath — but can't eliminate the last layer of fat covering it. The radiofrequency component of Emsculpt NEO targets that subcutaneous fat layer directly, and the HIFEM component builds the muscle beneath it simultaneously.

Flanks and love handles are the second most common target. Men tend to store fat in the abdominal and flank region preferentially, and this is one of those areas where consistent diet and exercise produce frustratingly inconsistent results. Treating the flanks with Emsculpt NEO reduces the fat while also strengthening the underlying obliques — which creates a more defined waist, not just less bulk.

Arms round out the top three. Bicep and tricep definition — particularly the separation between muscle heads that's notoriously hard to achieve through training alone — responds well to the HIFEM applicators.

How Male Physiology Responds

The HIFEM electromagnetic energy doesn't distinguish between male and female muscle tissue. What differs is body composition — and for most men, it works in your favor.

Men typically carry more lean muscle mass and lower average body fat percentages than women. That means the muscle-building effect of Emsculpt NEO is often more immediately visible on male patients. There's less fat tissue between the muscle and the skin surface, so when a treated muscle hypertrophies, you see the definition sooner. Testosterone also supports the hypertrophy response — your body's anabolic environment helps it capitalize on the stimulus the treatment creates.

The practical upshot: men who are already reasonably fit often see visible definition changes faster than the standard 8–12 week timeline suggests for the average patient. Leaner starting points produce earlier visible results.

For men who are already training but have plateaued on definition: Emsculpt NEO creates a type of muscle stimulus the gym can't replicate — roughly 20,000 forced supramaximal contractions per 30-minute session. It doesn't replace training. It does push past adaptation plateaus.

One Thing Worth Disclosing: Hernia Mesh

This comes up regularly with male patients seeking abdominal treatment, and it's worth knowing upfront.

Polypropylene mesh — the type used in the majority of modern hernia repairs — is not a contraindication. You can be treated safely.

Metal mesh is a contraindication for treatment in that area. HIFEM energy should not be applied over metal implants. If you've had hernia surgery and aren't sure which type of mesh was used, your surgeon's operative notes will show it. It takes one phone call to the surgical office to confirm, and it's worth doing before your consultation so we're not guessing.

The same principle applies to any other metal hardware near the intended treatment area — hip replacement components, surgical plates, and similar implants. Metal outside the direct treatment zone is generally fine; metal within it is not.

Training While in Treatment

Yes, you can keep going to the gym during your treatment series — and we'd prefer you do.

Staying active supports the fat-clearing process. Your lymphatic system clears the apoptotic fat cells more efficiently when you're moving regularly, and the muscle stimulus from training complements what Emsculpt NEO is doing. Think of it as stacking two types of muscle stimulus rather than choosing between them.

One practical note: avoid training the exact muscle group being treated in the 12–24 hours before a session. Fatigued muscle contracts less effectively under HIFEM — meaning you'll get a less intense session at lower productive intensity. Show up fresh for best results. After a session, expect soreness similar to delayed-onset muscle soreness (DOMS). That's normal, and it's a sign the treatment worked.

Who Gets the Best Results

Male patients who tend to see the most visible outcomes share a few characteristics:

  • Already reasonably active, with a BMI under 30 — Emsculpt NEO enhances a physique that's already heading in the right direction
  • Focused on one or two target areas rather than spreading sessions across too many zones at once
  • Willing to complete the full 4-session protocol and give results 8–12 weeks to fully develop
  • Looking for definition and body composition improvement, not significant scale weight loss

Men who are likely to be disappointed: anyone carrying significant excess weight and hoping Emsculpt NEO substitutes for dietary change, or anyone expecting dramatic results from a single session. As we've written elsewhere, this treatment works best as an optimizer — a finishing tool, not a foundation. That holds regardless of gender.
