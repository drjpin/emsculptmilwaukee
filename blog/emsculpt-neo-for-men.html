Emsculpt NEO for Men:
What to Expect and Who It's Actually For

By Bay View Chiropractic  ·  5 min read

← Back to Blog

Men make up a growing share of our Emsculpt NEO patients at Bay View Chiropractic — and the reasons they come in are pretty consistent. It's usually one of three things: stubborn love handles that don't budge no matter how clean they eat, abdominal definition that's almost there but hidden under a layer of fat, or muscle mass that's hard to maintain as they get into their 40s and 50s.

Body contouring has historically been marketed almost entirely to women. That's changing. So let's talk about what Emsculpt NEO actually does for male patients — including where it delivers and where it falls short.

The Two Areas Men Treat Most

Abdomen and love handles

The abdomen is the most common treatment area for men, and it makes sense why. Emsculpt NEO's combination of radiofrequency fat reduction and HIFEM muscle stimulation targets both problems at once — the subcutaneous fat layer sitting over the muscles and the muscles themselves. For a man who exercises regularly but carries a persistent soft layer around his midsection, that dual approach is more effective than anything that targets only fat or only muscle in isolation.

Love handles — the flanks, technically — are almost always treated alongside the abdomen. They're one of the most genetically stubborn fat deposits for men, and they respond well to the radiofrequency component of Emsculpt NEO. The fat destruction is permanent; those cells don't come back. What you do with your lifestyle after treatment determines how lasting the result feels.

Arms

Some male patients come in specifically wanting to improve arm definition — biceps and triceps. Emsculpt NEO can be applied to the arms, though it's less commonly requested than the abdomen. The muscle-building effect is real here, but the fat layer on most men's arms tends to be thinner than the abdomen, so the need for significant fat reduction is less common.

Why Men Often Respond Well to the Muscle-Building Component

Here's something that doesn't get discussed much: men who already train tend to respond particularly well to the HIFEM muscle stimulus. If you already have developed abdominal or core musculature from years of lifting or active work, the forced supramaximal contractions from Emsculpt NEO build on that existing foundation. You're not starting from scratch — you're pushing past a plateau.

The treatment induces roughly 20,000 muscle contractions per session. That's not a marketing number — it's a real neurological load that produces measurable hypertrophy and increased muscle fiber density over the 4-session series. Clinical studies show an average of 16% increase in muscle mass in treated areas. For men who hit a wall with conventional training, that's a meaningful stimulus.

Keep in mind: Emsculpt NEO builds on what you already have. If you're sedentary and significantly overweight, it's not a replacement for lifestyle change. It's most effective for men who are already reasonably active but can't get past a specific sticking point.

What the Treatment Actually Feels Like

This is a question we get from almost every male patient — often phrased as "is it going to hurt?" The honest answer: it's intense, but it's not painful in a sharp or burning way.

During the HIFEM phase, your muscles contract involuntarily and forcefully. It feels bizarre at first — your abdomen tightening and releasing in a rapid rhythm that you have no control over. Most people describe it as deeply uncomfortable for the first few minutes, then something you adjust to. By the second or third session, patients usually tell us it feels more like an aggressive workout than anything else.

The radiofrequency heating produces a warm sensation, similar to a heating pad but more focused. It shouldn't feel hot or burning — if it does, the intensity should be adjusted. Each session runs about 30 minutes per area. You walk out, go back to work, and the main thing you'll notice the next day is muscle soreness, similar to a hard gym session.

Who This Works Best For

The male patients who tend to be most satisfied with Emsculpt NEO results share a few things in common:

  • They exercise regularly but have hit a plateau with specific body composition goals
  • Their BMI is under 35 — above that, the HIFEM energy has a harder time penetrating to the muscle layer through thicker subcutaneous fat
  • They're targeting a specific area, not trying to lose overall body weight
  • They understand results continue developing for up to 3 months after the final session — they're not expecting instant change

Realistic Expectations

The clinical averages — 30% fat reduction and 25% muscle increase in treated areas — are meaningful numbers, but they're averages. Some men see more. Some see less. What we consistently hear from male patients who complete the full 4-session protocol is that the love handles are noticeably reduced and the abdominal definition they couldn't quite achieve through training alone is finally visible.

What we don't hear: "I lost 20 pounds." That's not what this treatment does. The full treatment protocol is body composition work, not weight loss. If your goal is significant scale movement, that conversation starts somewhere else.

We see patients from across Milwaukee, the south side, Wauwatosa, Greenfield, and Brookfield — men in their 30s, 40s, and 50s who work hard and want their body to reflect that effort. For the right candidate, Emsculpt NEO closes that gap.
