Emsculpt NEO for Men:
What to Expect

By Bay View Chiropractic  ·  6 min read

← Back to Blog

Men make up a significant portion of our Emsculpt NEO patients in Milwaukee — and the number keeps growing. That said, most of the before-and-after photos online still feature women, and most of the marketing seems aimed at them. So let's talk about how this treatment actually applies to men, what results look like, and where it fits in your routine.

Men and Body Composition Goals Are Different

This isn't controversial. Men typically carry fat differently than women — more concentrated in the abdomen and flanks, less in the hips and thighs. Men also tend to have more lean muscle mass to begin with, and their aesthetic goals are often framed around definition and strength rather than slimming.

That matters for Emsculpt NEO because the treatment does two distinct things: it builds muscle and reduces fat. For men, the muscle-building piece is often the more compelling part. Achieving visible abdominal definition takes either extremely low body fat or well-developed muscle — and usually both. Emsculpt NEO pushes the muscle side of that equation hard.

Where Men Typically Treat

The most common treatment areas we see in male patients:

Abdomen

This is the top request. Men who work out consistently but can't seem to get that sharp abdominal definition are good candidates. The HIFEM energy forces muscle contractions at an intensity and depth that's genuinely hard to replicate through conventional exercise. The result is denser, more defined abdominal musculature — which shows through the skin more clearly even without dramatic fat loss.

Flanks (Love Handles)

The obliques and the fat that sits above them are stubbornly resistant for a lot of men. Emsculpt NEO can target the flank area with applicators that address both the fat layer and the underlying oblique musculature. It won't eliminate a large amount of fat, but for men who are already close to where they want to be, it can make a real difference in that side profile.

Arms

Biceps and triceps treatment is less talked about but genuinely effective. If your arms look soft despite regular lifting, or if you've hit a plateau you can't push past, Emsculpt NEO stimulation of those muscle groups can help break through it.

Buttocks

Men ask about this less, but we do treat it. The glutes are the largest muscle group in the body, and building them has functional benefits beyond aesthetics — especially for patients with low back pain or hip instability. That's an area where our chiropractic background actually adds clinical context to the treatment.

Practical note: Men generally tolerate higher treatment intensities than women during the muscle contraction phase. We push intensity as high as the patient can comfortably handle — and many male patients can go higher than average. Higher intensity typically means better muscle recruitment and better outcomes.

What the Results Look Like for Men

Be realistic about what Emsculpt NEO can and can't do. Clinical studies consistently show about a 25% increase in muscle thickness in the treated area and around 30% reduction in subcutaneous fat after a standard four-session series. Those numbers are meaningful, but they're body composition improvements — not dramatic physical transformations visible from across the room.

For men who are already in decent shape, those improvements tend to show up clearly. You're not building from zero; you're sharpening definition that's already developing. The guy who runs three times a week and lifts twice a week but has a stubborn belly that won't fully lean out is exactly the profile where results are most visible.

For men who are significantly overweight or who haven't been active, Emsculpt NEO is not the right starting point. We cover this honestly in our post on whether Emsculpt NEO is actually worth it — but the short version is that this is a body composition optimizer, not a weight loss treatment.

How It Fits With Your Training

One question we get often: can you keep working out during your treatment series? Yes. You don't need to stop training. The only caveat is that you'll experience muscle soreness after sessions — similar to delayed onset muscle soreness after a heavy workout. We recommend not hammering the same muscle groups with heavy resistance training the day after a session. Give those muscles 24–48 hours to recover before you load them again.

Some patients actually find the forced contraction sessions complement their lifting by producing a stimulus their regular training doesn't reach — particularly the deep muscle fibers that compound lifts can miss.

You'll also want to understand the results timeline going in. The full benefit builds over two to three months after your last session as your body remodels the muscle tissue and the fat reduction completes. Don't expect a dramatic change immediately after session one.

What About Other Treatments?

Some men ask about combining Emsculpt NEO with CoolSculpting or whether one is better than the other for male body composition. They serve different purposes. Emsculpt NEO builds muscle and reduces fat simultaneously. CoolSculpting only reduces fat and has no effect on muscle. For men whose primary goal is definition — not just fat removal — Emsculpt NEO addresses both levers at once. Our comparison piece on Emsculpt NEO vs. CoolSculpting goes deeper on this if you're weighing both options.

Is There a Downside?

Cost is the main one. A four-session series for a single treatment area is a meaningful investment. If you're treating abs and flanks separately, that's two series. We'll be upfront about pricing during your consultation so you can make an informed decision before committing.

There are also contraindications that apply to everyone regardless of gender: metal implants in the treatment area, pacemakers, certain medications, and active inflammatory conditions. Nothing unusual — these are standard to any high-energy body treatment — but worth discussing during your consultation.

Recovery is minimal. Sessions run about 30 minutes. You can go back to work the same day. The muscle soreness is real but manageable, and it usually peaks about 24 hours after each session.

The Honest Bottom Line for Men

Emsculpt NEO isn't a shortcut. If your diet is off and you're not active, no treatment is going to do the heavy lifting for you. But if you're already putting in the work and you're stuck at a plateau — or if you have a specific area that just won't cooperate no matter what you do — this is a clinically grounded tool that actually does what it says.
