How Much Does Emsculpt NEO
Cost in Milwaukee?

Emsculpt NEO at Bay View Chiropractic in Milwaukee is $650 per session. The price is the same for every treatment area, and it does not change from month to month. A standard series of 4 sessions for one area is $2,600.

Most Emsculpt NEO providers ask you to book a consultation before they will tell you a price. We would rather you know the number before you pick up the phone.

Emsculpt NEO Price List

  • Single session: $650
  • Series of 4 sessions, one treatment area: $2,600
  • Two treatment areas, 4 sessions each: $5,200
  • Maintenance session: $650
  • Consultation: Free

What a Session Includes

One session is a 30-minute Emsculpt NEO treatment on one area: abdomen, buttocks, arms, hips, inner thighs, or flanks. We have all three applicator sizes (large, small, and edge), so the price does not change based on which applicator your treatment area needs.

There is no downtime. You can go back to work or the gym right after your appointment.

How Many Sessions You Will Pay For

The clinical protocol is 4 sessions per treatment area, spaced 5 to 10 days apart. That is the schedule the published results are based on: an average 25% increase in muscle and 30% reduction in fat in the treated area.

You are not required to buy all 4 up front. If budget is the deciding factor, we recommend finishing 4 sessions on one priority area before starting a second area. You can read more about that in how many Emsculpt NEO treatments you need.

The short version: $650 per session, 4 sessions per area, $2,600 for a full series. Free consultation. No package requirement.

Does Insurance Cover Emsculpt NEO?

No. Emsculpt NEO for body contouring is an elective treatment and is not covered by health insurance. Payment plans are available, and you can ask about them during your consultation.
